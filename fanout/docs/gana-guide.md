# Fanout: how to use it

Fanout takes one video and posts it to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn, each in the right shape and with its own caption.

## The first time (about 10 minutes)

1. Open the link you were sent and log in.
2. Go to **Platforms** and follow the four steps under **Connect Buffer**. Buffer is a free service that keeps your social media logins; Fanout posts through it.
3. Still on **Platforms**, check each platform:
   - The switch turns a platform on or off.
   - **Post with** shows which account it posts to. "Practice only" means it prepares the post but doesn't publish it.
   - **Hashtags for this platform** are added to every post there. The grey **Example post** box shows exactly how it will look.
4. Under **Vertical framing**, choose **Fill the screen** (trims the sides of wide videos) or **Show the whole video** (adds bars).

## Every time you post

1. Go to **Videos**.
2. Drop your video in the box (or click it to choose one). Use your normal export; Fanout makes the vertical versions.
3. Write the caption once, then press **Post it**.
4. Watch the card: each platform shows **Posting…**, then **Posted ✓** once Buffer has accepted it. Buffer publishes it within moments; check the post in each app.

## If something says "Couldn't post"

The card tells you why in plain words, for example "Too short for this platform: it needs at least 3 seconds." Fix the cause if needed, then press **Try again**. Try again only appears on posts that didn't go out.

## Good to know

- **Practice: not posted** means that platform isn't connected yet, so nothing went out.
- Videos are checked automatically; if a file can't be used, Fanout says so right after uploading.
- The **Activity log** at the bottom of the page is a detailed record, useful when asking for help.
